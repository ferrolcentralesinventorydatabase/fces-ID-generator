Print batch 0 selected

Cards print true to size (53.98 × 85.6mm — standard ATM/ID card). 6 per A4 sheet, with cut guides.

Tip: print the front sheets first. Then take that same paper and flip each sheet over left-to-right, like turning a page in a book (keep the top edge as the top edge), and feed it back into the printer to print the backs — this keeps every card's back aligned with its own front.

Fine-tune BACK alignment

Every printer feeds re-inserted paper a little differently, so the back sheet can land a fraction of a millimeter off even though the layout itself is mirrored correctly. Look for the 4 faint crosshair marks now printed in the corners of every sheet — hold a front and back sheet together up to the light; any gap between the crosshairs is the true registration error. Nudge here to close that gap, then reprint the BACK sheet.
